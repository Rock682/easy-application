Posts and Eligibility

The 2026 recruitment bulletin covers School Assistant and Secondary Grade Teacher posts in Government, Zilla Parishad, Mandal Parishad, Municipality and Municipal Corporation schools. Post-wise and district-wise vacancies, subject combinations, required teacher-training qualifications and TET conditions must be checked in the official bulletin and vacancy tables.

Do not rely on generic qualification summaries: eligibility differs by post, subject and reservation category. Read the relevant post-specific qualification table before paying the fee.

How to Apply

  1. Open the official AP DSC 2026 portal.
  2. Read the Information Bulletin and the post-wise vacancy/qualification details.
  3. Complete fee payment and online application between 11 and 30 November 2026.
  4. Enter personal, educational, TET and reservation details exactly as shown on certificates.
  5. Upload the required photograph, signature and documents, then submit the application.
  6. Save the submitted application and payment receipt; monitor the portal for exam updates.
