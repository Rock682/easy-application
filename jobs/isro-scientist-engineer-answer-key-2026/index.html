ISRO Scientist/Engineer Answer Key 2026: Latest Update

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) has activated the answer-key and objection facility for the Scientist/Engineer 'SC' Computer-Based Test under Advertisement No. ISRO:ICRB:03(EMC):2026. Candidates who appeared for the examination can sign in to the official candidate portal to view the provisional answer key and their recorded responses.

ISRO states that the portal will remain available from 12 November 2026 at 1:00 PM until 15 November 2026 at 1:00 PM. Candidates who identify a discrepancy should submit their challenge within this period. Objections sent through any other mode will not be considered.

How to Check the ISRO Answer Key 2026

  1. Visit the official ISRO candidate portal using the link above.
  2. Open the login page for Advertisement No. ISRO:ICRB:03(EMC):2026.
  3. Enter the credentials requested by the portal.
  4. Open the provisional answer key and recorded response sheet.
  5. Check each response against the corresponding provisional answer.
  6. Download or save the available documents for your records.

How to Raise an Objection Against the ISRO Answer Key

  1. Sign in to the official objection management portal.
  2. Review the question and answer carefully before raising a challenge.
  3. Select the question or answer you want to challenge and follow the instructions shown on the portal.
  4. Provide the explanation and supporting information requested by the portal.
  5. Submit the objection before 15 November 2026 at 1:00 PM.
  6. Save the submission confirmation for future reference.

Follow the instructions and any applicable fee or evidence requirements displayed on the official portal. This article does not assume an objection fee or submission rule that is not stated in the official notice.

What Happens After the Objection Window?

After the objection period closes, ISRO will process the challenges in accordance with its recruitment procedure. Candidates should monitor the official ISRO recruitment page for any revised or final answer key, further examination updates and interview-related announcements. Do not treat an estimated score calculated from the provisional key as an official result.
