Eligibility and Selection

This CEN is for undergraduate-level posts. Candidates must satisfy the 12th-standard qualification, age, medical and post-specific requirements in the detailed notification. Typing skill tests apply to the relevant clerk/typist posts. The selection process and document requirements are governed by CEN 07/2026.

How to Apply

  1. Read the detailed CEN on the official RRB website.
  2. Open the RRB Apply portal and select the appropriate RRB.
  3. Complete registration and enter personal, educational and post-preference details.
  4. Upload the prescribed photograph, signature and certificates.
  5. Pay the applicable fee and submit before 13 November 2026, 11:59 PM.
  6. Save the application confirmation and payment receipt.
