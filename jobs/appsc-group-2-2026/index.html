Vacancies and Posts

Notification 37/2026 invites applications for various categories of Group-II executive and non-executive posts. The post-wise vacancy count, category reservation, pay scale and service details must be read from the detailed notification and its annexures. Do not rely on unofficial vacancy summaries where the post-wise table is incomplete.

Eligibility

Educational qualification and age limits differ by post. Candidates should verify the exact degree/subject requirements, age reckoning date, local status, reservation and relaxation rules in Notification 37/2026 before applying. A general Group-II eligibility summary is not a substitute for the post-wise eligibility table.

Important Dates

  • Notification date: 10 November 2026
  • Online application begins: 10 November 2026
  • Last date to submit online application: 11 December 2026, 11:59 PM
  • Exam date and hall-ticket schedule: To be announced by APPSC

How to Apply

  1. Open the official APPSC portal.
  2. Read the detailed Notification 37/2026 and web note in full.
  3. Complete or update One Time Profile Registration (OTPR), if required.
  4. Open the Group-II application link and enter the requested details.
  5. Check post-wise eligibility, local status, category and certificates before payment.
  6. Submit the application before 11 December 2026 at 11:59 PM and save the acknowledgement.
