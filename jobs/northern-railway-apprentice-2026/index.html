Northern Railway Apprentice Recruitment 2026: Overview

The Railway Recruitment Cell (RRC), Northern Railway has invited online applications for engagement of 4,651 Act Apprentices for the 2026–27 training year. The engagement is being made under the Apprentices Act, 1961, at various divisions, units and workshops of Northern Railway.

Applications opened on 11 November 2026. Eligible candidates must submit the online form by 11 December 2026 at 11:59 PM. The notified training slots are provisional and may change according to administrative requirements.

Important: Completing apprenticeship training does not give a candidate an automatic right to a permanent job or absorption in Indian Railways. The notification expressly states that the Railway is not obligated to offer employment after training.

Slots are distributed by trade and individual unit/workshop. Candidates must check the official Annexure-A and select a cluster and unit that actually lists training slots for their ITI trade. Applying for a unit without a slot in the candidate's trade can lead to rejection.

Educational Qualification

  • Passed SSC/Matriculation/Class 10 or equivalent under the 10+2 system from a recognised board.
  • Must have at least 50% aggregate marks in Class 10.
  • Must have passed ITI in the relevant trade, issued by an NCVT/SCVT-recognised institution recognised by the Government of India.
  • Both the Class 10 and ITI results must already have been declared by 6 November 2026, the notification date. Candidates awaiting either result on that date are not eligible.

Age Limit and Relaxation

The candidate must have completed 15 years and must not have completed 24 years of age as on 11 December 2026.

  • SC/ST: upper-age relaxation of 5 years.
  • OBC: upper-age relaxation of 3 years.
  • PwBD: upper-age relaxation of 10 years.
  • Ex-servicemen: relaxation as specified in the detailed notification.

Applicants claiming reservation or age relaxation must possess the prescribed certificates in the required format.

Application Fee

The application fee is ₹100, payable online as part of the application process. SC, ST, PwBD and women candidates are exempt from the fee. Online transaction charges, if any, are payable by the candidate.

Selection Process and Merit List

There will be no written examination or viva/interview. RRC will prepare a merit list by calculating the average percentage of marks obtained in Class 10 and ITI, giving equal weightage to both qualifications. Merit lists will be prepared cluster-wise, trade-wise and community-wise.

Applicants should enter marks exactly as shown in their certificates. The notification explains how marks/grades and CGPA are to be converted. A discrepancy between application details and original certificates can lead to rejection. The expected date for display of the merit list is February 2027.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official RRC Northern Railway website at rrcnr.org.
  2. Open the notice titled “Engagement of 4651 Apprentices under the Apprentices Act 1961” and read the notification and Annexure-A.
  3. Check the trade-wise slots and choose only a cluster/unit where your ITI trade is notified.
  4. Use the “Engagement of Act Apprentice” online application link published on the official RRC website and complete candidate registration.
  5. Log in and fill in personal, educational, category and trade details accurately.
  6. Upload the required photograph, signature, thumb impression and certificates in the prescribed format.
  7. Pay the applicable fee online, if required, and submit the application.
  8. Save or print the completed application and payment receipt for your records.

The notification permits only one application per candidate. It also states that changes cannot be made after final submission, so check all details carefully before submitting.

Documents to Keep Ready

  • Class 10 certificate/marksheet as proof of date of birth and qualification.
  • ITI trade certificate and consolidated marksheet.
  • Category certificate, where applicable.
  • PwBD or ex-servicemen certificate, where applicable.
  • Recent photograph, signature and thumb impression in the required file format and size.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many apprentice slots are available?

There are 4,651 provisional Act Apprentice training slots across six Northern Railway clusters.

What is the qualification for Northern Railway Apprentice 2026?

Applicants need Class 10 with at least 50% aggregate marks and an ITI certificate in the relevant trade from an NCVT/SCVT-recognised institution.

Is there a written examination?

No. Selection is based on a merit list using Class 10 and ITI marks with equal weightage. There is no written test or viva.

What is the last date to apply?

The online application closes on 11 December 2026 at 11:59 PM.

Does apprenticeship guarantee a permanent railway job?

No. The notification clearly states that apprenticeship training does not confer a right to absorption or permanent employment in Northern Railway.

Where can I apply?

Visit the official RRC Northern Railway website at rrcnr.org and use the Act Apprentice application link published there.
