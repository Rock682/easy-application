What is AP ICET and who should use this guide?

AP ICET is the Andhra Pradesh Integrated Common Entrance Test for eligible candidates seeking MBA and MCA admissions in Andhra Pradesh. AP ICET 2026 is conducted by Andhra University, Visakhapatnam, on behalf of APSCHE.

This guide explains the completed 2026 exam cycle, the official result and rank-card services, score validity and the next admission steps candidates should verify. APSCHE and the official admission portal remain the final authority for counselling schedules, institutions, seat availability and admission rules.

How to Check AP ICET 2026 Result and Download the Rank Card

Use only the official AP ICET portal. The official result page asks for your registration number and ICET hall-ticket number. The official rank-card page asks for your registration number, ICET hall-ticket number and date of birth.

  1. Open the official result or rank-card service.
  2. Enter the requested details exactly as used for your application.
  3. Check the information displayed before saving your result or rank card.
  4. Keep a copy of the rank card and relevant AP ICET documents for future official admission procedures.

If the official service is unavailable or your details do not work, use the contact details on the official portal rather than sharing credentials with unofficial websites.

What to Do After AP ICET 2026 Results

Your AP ICET result is an important step, but a rank alone does not automatically complete admission. Candidates should follow the official APSCHE admission or counselling process for the 2026–27 academic year.

Save official documents

Keep your application details, hall ticket and rank card available and follow official instructions for any additional documents.

Verify course eligibility

Meeting AP ICET requirements does not remove the need to satisfy the eligibility rules applicable to your chosen MBA or MCA programme and the official admission process.

Watch official counselling announcements

Check APSCHE and the official admission portal for counselling registration, certificate verification, web options, seat allotment and deadlines when announced.

Check institutions and courses carefully

Verify the current official list of eligible institutions, programmes, seats and admission conditions before making choices.

Eligibility — MBA and MCA Explained

For MBA

Read the official instruction booklet and applicable admission rules for the qualifying-degree and minimum-mark requirements. Qualifying for AP ICET does not automatically establish eligibility for every MBA programme or institution.

For MCA

MCA candidates should pay particular attention to the Mathematics requirement and verify whether their academic record satisfies the official criteria. Check the official rules rather than relying only on the name of your degree.

Exam Pattern and Understanding Your Score

AP ICET tests Analytical Ability, Mathematical Ability and Communication Ability. Candidates should use the official instruction booklet for the complete syllabus and examination rules.

The official 2026 instruction booklet states that the rank is valid for admissions for the 2026–27 academic year, subject to applicable admission rules.
